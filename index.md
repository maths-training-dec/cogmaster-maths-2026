Welcome! This page is your home base for the crash course. You'll find all the
lecture notes, slides, exercise sheets, and solutions here, organised by session.
Bookmark it — we'll keep it updated throughout the course.

## The two tracks

The course runs in two parallel tracks so that everyone works at a comfortable level:

- [**Basic track**]({{ '/basic/' | relative_url }}) — builds the foundations from
  scratch, assuming no prior mathematics. Taught by **Ali**.
- [**Advanced track**]({{ '/advanced/' | relative_url }}) — a faster recap for
  students with a mathematics background. Taught by **Sara**.

Not sure which track you're in? You'll be placed based on the short
[placement questionnaire](https://forms.gle/EVgsYC1k9TL1rZ4QA){:target="_blank"} —
and we'll confirm your group by email.

## How to use this page

Use the sidebar (or the search box at the top) to navigate. Each session page lists
the topics covered and the materials to download. For the Basic track, the full set
of lecture notes and slides is available on the
[Basic track overview]({{ '/basic/' | relative_url }}), and each session has its own
exercise sheet and solutions.
