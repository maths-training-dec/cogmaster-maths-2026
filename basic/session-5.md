# Session 5 - Differential Equations

The analysis block in one sitting: functions, derivatives, integrals, and the one
differential equation that models learning and forgetting.

## Topics covered

- What a function is; the exponential and its time constant
- Derivatives: the slope, the rules, and reading the sign
- Integrals: adding change back up, and the fundamental theorem
- Functions of several variables: contour maps and partial derivatives
- Vector-valued functions: a path and its velocity
- The linear differential equation and its stability
- The learning and forgetting curves
