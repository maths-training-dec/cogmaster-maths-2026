# Session 3 — Analysis I

The start of the analysis block. We begin from what a function actually is, build
up derivatives and integrals as tools you can use rather than rules you recite,
and end on the one differential equation that everything later rests on:
$\dot x = ax + b$.

The recurring examples are the **forgetting curve** and the **learning curve** —
the same equation, run in two directions.

**Length:** ~110 min, with a practice break in each block.

## Topics covered

### Functions

- What a function is
- Five shapes cover almost everything you will meet
- The **exponential** and the **time constant** $\tau$
- The forgetting curve $R' = -R/\tau$

### Derivatives

- The derivative is a **slope**
- The rules: power, product, quotient, chain
- The mistake to head off (before it becomes a habit)
- What the **sign** tells you — often all you need

### Integrals

- The integral adds the changes back up
- The **fundamental theorem**: differentiation and integration undo each other
- Two techniques, and that is all we need here

### A first differential equation

- **Solving is not integrating** — and the error worth naming out loud
- The equation the whole course rests on: $\dot x = ax + b$, its fixed point
  $x^\star$ and its exponential solution
- **Stability is the sign of a slope**: $a < 0$ settles, $a > 0$ runs away
- The learning curve — the forgetting curve upside down. Both say "the rate is
  proportional to the distance still to go"
- Simulating with **Euler's method**: look up how fast you are moving, move at
  that speed for a short while, look again
