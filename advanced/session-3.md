# Session 3 — Analysis I

Functions, derivatives, and integrals as usable tools, ending on the one
differential equation everything later rests on.

## Topics covered

- Functions, the exponential, and the time constant
- Derivatives: the rules, and what the sign tells you
- Integrals and the fundamental theorem
- A first differential equation: its fixed point and stability
- The learning and forgetting curves
- Simulating with Euler's method
