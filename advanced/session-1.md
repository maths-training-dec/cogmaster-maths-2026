# Session 1 - Linear Algebra I

Everything in linear algebra built from two operations, adding and scaling vectors,
up to the matrix as a transformation of space.

## Topics covered

- Vectors, linear combinations, span, and linear independence
- The dot product: norm, angle, and cosine similarity
- Projection: the shadow and the residual
- Orthonormal bases and change of basis
- Matrices as transformations; the columns as where the axes land
- The data matrix, and applying a scoring rule to everyone at once
