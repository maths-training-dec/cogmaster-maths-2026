# Session 4 — Analysis II

Adding dimensions: several inputs, several outputs, and several equations at once,
where the linear algebra pays off a second time.

## Topics covered

- Functions of several variables: contour maps and partial derivatives
- The gradient and gradient descent; critical points
- Vector-valued functions: trajectories, velocity, and the Jacobian
- Systems of differential equations: one mode per eigenvector
- Stability from the eigenvalues (node, saddle, spiral, centre)
- Nonlinear systems: linearising at a fixed point, and nullclines
