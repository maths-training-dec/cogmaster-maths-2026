# Session 2 - Linear Algebra II

Rank, eigenvectors, PCA, and the SVD: how many dimensions a data table really has,
and what varies together.

## Topics covered

- The determinant, the inverse, and rank
- Collinearity and rank-one structure
- Eigenvectors, eigenvalues, and diagonalisation
- Complex eigenvalues as rotation
- Symmetric matrices, covariance, and PCA
- The singular value decomposition and low-rank approximation
