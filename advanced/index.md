# Advanced track

A faster recap for students who already have a mathematics background. The lectures
are interactive, with examples worked at the board and short exercises along the way.

Two blocks of two sessions each, linear algebra and analysis, followed by a
probability and statistics refresher.

## Sessions

1. [Linear Algebra I]({{ '/advanced/session-1/' | relative_url }}): vectors, the dot product, orthonormal bases, and matrices as transformations
2. [Linear Algebra II]({{ '/advanced/session-2/' | relative_url }}): determinant, rank, eigenvectors, PCA, and the SVD
3. [Analysis I]({{ '/advanced/session-3/' | relative_url }}): functions, derivatives, integrals, and a first differential equation
4. [Analysis II]({{ '/advanced/session-4/' | relative_url }}): several variables, the gradient and Jacobian, and systems of differential equations
5. [Probability & Statistics]({{ '/advanced/session-5/' | relative_url }}): a refresher of distributions, inference, and Bayes, with a glimpse of modelling

Use the sidebar to jump to any session.
