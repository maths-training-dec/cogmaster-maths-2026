# Session 5 — Analysis (basic track)

The **basic-track** counterpart of Sessions 3–4: the whole analysis block in one
sitting. Nothing beyond arithmetic is assumed. Every rule is stated, used on a
number, and practised straight away.

It is self-contained — you do not need Sessions 1–4 to follow it. Compared with
the advanced sessions, the pace is slower and the scope is smaller: one ordinary
linear differential equation, and no systems of them.

The recurring examples are the **forgetting curve**, the **learning curve**, and a
**mouse-tracking path**.

**Length:** ~110 min, with a practice break in each of the five blocks.

## Topics covered

### Functions and the exponential

- What a function is
- Three shapes are almost all we need
- The **exponential** and the **time constant**
- The forgetting curve

### Derivatives

- The derivative is a **slope**
- The rules
- The mistake to head off
- Often the **sign** is all you need

### Integrals

- The integral adds the changes back up
- Integration is differentiation, backwards

### Functions of several variables

- More than one input
- The **contour map**
- **Partial derivatives**: vary one input, hold the others still
- Functions whose **output** is a vector: a path, and its velocity — a
  mouse-tracking cursor position $(x(t), y(t))$ that bends toward the unchosen
  option is a statement about velocity

### Differential equations

- The question, in words before symbols
- Why the **exponential**, and nothing else
- Adding a constant push: $\dot x = ax + b$
- The learning curve
